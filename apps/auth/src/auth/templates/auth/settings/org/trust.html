About Trust Policies

Trust policies control which users can sign in to your organization. This is useful for multi-tenant environments where you want to restrict access based on where users registered.

  • Strict (None): Only users who created their account on this specific organization can sign in
  • Members Only: Only organization members can sign in, regardless of registration origin
  • Specific Tenants: Users from selected trusted organizations (or members) can sign in
  • Open (All): Any authenticated user in the system can sign in

Note: Changing the trust mode may immediately affect who can access your organization.
