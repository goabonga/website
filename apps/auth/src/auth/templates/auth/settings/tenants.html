About Organizations

Organizations (tenants) allow companies to manage their users and configure single sign-on (SSO) with external identity providers like Google, GitHub, or corporate SAML/OIDC providers.

  • Owner: Full control over the organization, including managing other owners
  • Admin: Can manage members, IdPs, branding and templates (except owner roles)
  • Member: Basic access to organization resources
