Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any differences.

What happens if I exceed my limits?

You'll receive a notification when approaching limits. If exceeded, API calls will return an error until the next billing cycle or you upgrade.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Paid plans can include a free trial. When a plan has one, its length is shown when you subscribe.

How is storage calculated?

Storage includes your document content and generated vector embeddings. Metadata has minimal impact on storage.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Plans that are offered with annual billing show the saving next to their yearly price.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice.
