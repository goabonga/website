Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upgrade my plan? ▼
Click the upgrade button on your desired plan. You are sent to the secure payment page, or your subscription is changed with a proration when you already have one.
Can I downgrade my plan? ▼
Plan downgrades are handled manually to ensure your data is preserved. Please contact support to discuss downgrade options.
What happens if I exceed my limits? ▼
You'll receive a warning when approaching your limits. Once exceeded, new documents or API calls may be rejected until you upgrade or the next billing cycle begins.
Is there a free trial for paid plans? ▼
Some paid plans include a free trial. When a plan has one, its length is shown when you subscribe.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice.
